Our Story

We are students, just like you, on a mission to make learning collaborative and accessible for everyone at our college.

Our Mission

GCOEAvidNotes was born out of a simple idea: to create a single, centralized platform where students can find all the study materials they need without the last-minute hassle. We remember the frantic search for notes, previous year papers, and helpful video links right before exams. Our mission is to eliminate that stress by building a community-driven library of resources that is organized, reliable, and easy to access.

How It Started

It all started during a late-night study session. A group of us were struggling to find coherent notes for a tough subject. We realized that while many students had excellent materials, they were scattered across countless WhatsApp groups and personal drives. We decided to build a home for this content—a place where the collective knowledge of our peers could benefit everyone. That's how GCOEAvidNotes came to be.
